# Generation prompt set

The concepts were generated with the built-in image-generation tool in
`logo-brand` mode. Every prompt shared these requirements:

- early-stage native macOS application icon for a lightweight Markdown preview
  and Quick Look app;
- one centered, simple, vector-friendly geometric mark with restrained depth;
- large optical scale and generous margins, legible at 64–96 px;
- no words or labels inside the icon;
- avoid document sheets, eyes, lenses, magnifiers, pens, quills, arrows,
  M monograms, `md` wordmarks, butterflies, crystals, and close resemblance to
  Apple Preview, ChatGPT, QLMarkdown, Marked, MarkText, MacDown, Typora,
  Obsidian, iA Writer, Ulysses, Zettlr, and MarkEdit.

Per-concept requests:

1. **Rendered Fold:** three short parallel source strokes become one continuous
   coral folded ribbon and a clean rendered panel on deep aubergine.
2. **Split Signal:** an ivory grooved source pane and clean output pane separated
   by a slim diagonal seam on forest green.
3. **V Aperture:** two broad ochre slabs form a positive V around a narrow
   aperture; only the left slab carries three source grooves, on graphite.
4. **Hash Window:** a monochrome hash-inspired lattice with a small centered
   square aperture, charcoal on parchment/apricot.
5. **Render Portal:** three ivory lanes enter a rounded-square aperture and exit
   as one broad band, on brick red with a copper inner edge.
6. **Offset Pane:** a dark grooved rear lozenge and smooth ivory front lozenge
   interlock on terracotta without reading as paper sheets.
7. **Linebreak:** three saffron/orange lanes merge through one broad typographic
   bend into a single arm on ultramarine, without an arrowhead.
8. **Corner Window:** two opposing warm-ivory rounded corners frame a square of
   negative space on vermilion; only the lower-left corner has source grooves.
9. **Soft Merge:** three pale-mint source lines organically converge into one
   rounded result surface on wine red with a coral join.
10. **Markup Star:** a sturdy six-spoke burnt-orange asterisk with a hollow
    hexagonal center on petrol green; explicitly not an AI sparkle or gear.

Post-generation review rejected concept 10 because the result still overlaps
Zapier's established orange-asterisk brand language. The prompt is retained to
make the concept round reproducible, not as an endorsement of the output.
